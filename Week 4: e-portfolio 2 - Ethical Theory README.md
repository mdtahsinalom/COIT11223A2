## Artefact 1: What is Ethical Theory?
<img width="940" height="606" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd67b715-7d84-4896-9c7b-de69c24ee92b" />

**Video:** [Different Ethical Theories & Approaches](https://youtu.be/FRd8MmmkXs0?si=3oT6vszyL6ZYzbc1)

### Summary of the Artefact

This video provides an explanation of three major theories of ethics: **Utilitarianism, Deontological Ethics, and Virtue Ethics**. It demonstrates the difference between Utilitarianism, which emphasises the **"Greatest Good for the Greatest Number of People"**, and Deontological Ethics, which emphasises **"Moral Duty"** and/or **"Moral Rules"**. Virtue Ethics is about developing good virtues or character, such as being honest and fair. The video helped me realise that there are various ways of ethical thinking that can be applied to real-life situations to make better decisions.

### Justification on Why I Chose the Artefact

This artefact was selected because it makes it easy to understand the various ethical theories and the way they affect decision-making. This topic was relevant to my learning because Artificial Intelligence can cause many ethical issues, including privacy, fairness, bias, responsibility, and the effects of automated decisions on people.

The video provided an understanding of Utilitarianism, Deontological Ethics, and Virtue Ethics and helped me understand that there are different ways to determine whether an action is moral or immoral. I chose this artefact because it provided me with a deeper understanding of how ethical theories can be applied to real-world scenarios and how they can assist individuals in making more responsible decisions when using and developing AI technologies.

The article was interesting to me because AI systems can affect different groups of people in different ways. The article helped me understand why developers should consider fairness, inclusion, transparency, and responsibility when designing AI systems. ### Summary of the Artefact

This article discusses the relationship between **ethics and morality** and their significance in making responsible decisions. It covers different areas of ethics, including **descriptive ethics, normative ethics, meta-ethics, and applied ethics**.

The article also discusses ethical theories such as **virtue ethics, deontological ethics, and consequentialism**. It explains the importance of ethics in solving ethical dilemmas and making ethical decisions. Ethics is also becoming an important consideration in fields such as computers, robotics, and Artificial Intelligence.

Overall, this artefact helped me understand why ethical principles are important when developing and using new technologies.

### Justification on Why I Chose the Artefact

I selected this artefact because it explains ethics in detail and shows how ethics can affect human behaviour and decision-making. I found it interesting in relation to the development and application of AI because AI can raise ethical questions involving fairness, responsibility, privacy, and its effects on society.

The article helped me understand different ethical approaches and how they can be used to determine whether decisions or actions are right or wrong. I chose this artefact because it connects fundamental ethical concepts with contemporary technologies such as AI and helped me understand how technology should be developed and used responsibly and ethically.
