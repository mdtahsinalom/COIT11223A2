AI relies on computer systems, large amounts of data, and advanced algorithms to process information and complete tasks effectively.

# Artefact 3: Scholarly Article

## Source

Vieriu, A.M. and Petrea, G. (2025)  
**The Impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on Students’ Academic Development**

Available at:

https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/3/343

---

## Summary of the Artefact

This research article investigates how Artificial Intelligence affects students' learning experiences and academic development in higher education.

The study involved 85 second-year students from the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest. Data was collected through questionnaires about students' experiences with AI-based learning tools.

The findings show that AI can:

- Support personalised learning
- Increase student engagement
- Provide faster access to information
- Help students understand difficult concepts

However, the article also identifies several challenges:

- Overdependence on AI tools
- Reduced critical thinking skills
- Privacy concerns
- Academic integrity issues

The study concludes that AI can improve education when used responsibly with appropriate ethical guidelines.

---

## Why I Chose This Artefact

I chose this scholarly article because it relates directly to modern education and my own learning experience.

As AI tools become more common in universities, I wanted to understand their impact on students.

This article provided a balanced view of AI by explaining both the benefits and risks. It helped me understand that AI should support learning rather than replace independent thinking.
