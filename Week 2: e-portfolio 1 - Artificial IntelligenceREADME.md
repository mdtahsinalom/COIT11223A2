Overall, the artefact demonstrates that cyber security is equally as human, as well as planned and collaborative.

Artefact 3: Scholary Article  

 
https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/3/343
Summary of the aretfact
The artefact investigates how the use of artificial intelligence (AI) affects students' learning process and their results in higher education. The study was carried out on a sample of 85 second-year students at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest, through questionnaires, to obtain information on their experiences and opinions regarding the use of AI in learning. The findings indicate that AI can enhance personalised learning, boost student engagement, and deliver targeted support to students to help them reach optimal learning results with speedy access to information. There are also potential challenges in the use of AI in education, such as the risk of students relying excessively on AI, a decrease in critical thinking and problem-solving abilities, concerns regarding data privacy, and the potential for academic dishonesty. In summary, the artefact demonstrates that AI in education, if used responsibly, has the potential to be beneficial if supplemented with ethical guidelines and equitable proportions of AI to independent learning to ensure benefits outweigh risks.

Justification on why I chose the artefact
I selected this artefact because it explores a topic which is very pertinent to contemporary education and my own experiences of learning. In the age of increasing prevalence of AI in universities, I was eager to see the impact it has on student learning and their academic performance. Both the benefits – including personalised learning and enhanced engagement – and the drawbacks – such as reliance on AI, diminished critical thinking, privacy issues, and academic dishonesty – are illustrated. I chose this artefact as it offers a well-rounded view and informed by research, which led to a more balanced understanding of the opportunities and risks of using AI to enhance learning.
